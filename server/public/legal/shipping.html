Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

This Shipping and Delivery Policy explains how orders, digital credits, and services are fulfilled on Our Bloom (our-bloom.onrender.com).

1. 100% Digital Delivery

Our Bloom is a digital software application and relationship timeline service. We do not produce, warehouse, or ship physical tangible goods. Consequently, no physical shipping is involved in any transaction made through our platform.

2. Fulfillment Timeline & Method

  • Immediate Digital Fulfillment: All digital services, mobile app features, and voluntary Couple's Savings Vault deposits are delivered electronically and provisioned instantaneously upon payment confirmation from the payment gateway (PayU / UPI).
  • Confirmation Receipt: Upon successful transaction completion, a digital receipt and updated balance status is displayed immediately within the mobile application and recorded in your account transaction history.

3. Shipping Charges

Since all services and features are fulfilled electronically through the Our Bloom cloud network, there are zero shipping, handling, or delivery charges associated with any transaction on Our Bloom.

4. Delivery Issues or Delays

In the rare event that a network failure or banking gateway delay prevents your digital feature or vault credit from reflecting within 15 minutes of payment, please reach out to us with your 12-digit UPI UTR / Transaction ID. Our team will verify the payment and credit your account immediately.
