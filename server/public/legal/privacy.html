Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

Welcome to Our Bloom ("we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting the intimacy and privacy of couples using our mobile application and web services. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, protected, and disclosed when you visit our website (our-bloom.onrender.com) and use the Our Bloom mobile app.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide a private, connected, and delightful experience for you and your partner:

  • Account Information: Names, email addresses, profile pictures, relationship anniversary dates, and paired couple identifiers.
  • Shared Content: Love notes, memories, journal milestones, pinned romantic map locations, and customized game cards created by you for your partner.
  • Device & FCM Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration tokens used strictly to deliver real-time partner notifications, calls, and app update alerts.
  • Financial & Payment Transaction Data: When utilizing the Couple's Savings Vault or making digital deposits, we process payment confirmations, amount, timestamp, and 12-digit UPI Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) numbers. We never store your bank account passwords, UPI PINs, or raw credit/debit card credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To synchronize milestones, love notes, interactive games, and mutual savings between you and your partner.
  • To facilitate peer-to-peer real-time communication and audio/video calling.
  • To verify, credit, and record voluntary savings vault deposits via authorized payment channels.
  • To notify you of messages, notes, and milestones from your partner.
  • To comply with statutory financial regulations and Indian information technology guidelines.

3. Data Protection & Security

Your privacy is paramount. All data transmitted between your mobile devices and our servers is secured using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption (HTTPS / WSS). Data stored in Google Cloud Firestore and MongoDB is protected by role-based access control, cryptographic tokens, and strict multi-tenant isolation rules ensuring only you and your paired partner can read your shared memories.

4. Third-Party Services & Processors

We work with trusted industry providers for infrastructure and payment routing:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: For real-time database synchronization, push notifications, and media storage.
  • Payment Gateways (PayU / UPI): For processing voluntary deposits and payments. Payment processing complies with PCI-DSS standards.

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal memories, photos, notes, or relationship data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You may request the export or permanent deletion of your account and all associated shared milestones, notes, and photos at any time by contacting our support team.
