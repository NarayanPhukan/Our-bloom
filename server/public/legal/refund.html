Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

At Our Bloom, we are committed to complete transparency, fairness, and customer satisfaction regarding all digital services, premium features, and savings vault transactions on our platform (our-bloom.onrender.com).

1. Digital Vault Deposits & Withdrawals

Deposits made into the Couple's Savings Vault represent mutual savings managed between you and your paired partner:

  • Custody of Savings: Your deposited funds remain your mutual property. You can request to withdraw or cash out your saved funds to your registered bank account at any time via the Withdrawal Request screen in the mobile app.
  • Withdrawal Processing: Verified withdrawal requests are reviewed and settled directly to your designated bank account (joint or individual) within 24 to 48 business hours.

2. Accidental, Erroneous, or Duplicate Transactions

We understand that payment errors can happen:

  • If you make an accidental deposit, an incorrect amount, or experience a duplicate payment due to a network glitch, you may request a direct refund within 7 days of the transaction date.
  • To initiate a refund, please send an email to support@ourbloom.app with:
    • Your registered account email address
    • Transaction Date & Time
    • Amount in INR (₹)
    • The 12-digit UPI UTR / Reference number from your bank or payment app receipt

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Once your refund request is verified by our team:

  • The refund will be approved and initiated within 24 business hours.
  • The refund amount will be credited back directly to the original payment source (bank account / UPI VPA) within 5 to 7 working days, adhering to standard banking and payment gateway (PayU / NPCI) turnaround times.

4. Cancellation of Digital Subscriptions

If you purchase any digital subscription or premium feature pass within Our Bloom, you may cancel at any time. Cancellations take effect immediately or at the conclusion of the active billing cycle. If you are dissatisfied with a digital feature within the first 48 hours of purchase, you may contact support for a full refund.
